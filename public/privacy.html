Privacy Policy

Lyric Chord Composer · Last updated October 4, 2026

Lyric Chord Composer (“the app”) is a free, open-source web app for writing songs with lyrics, chords and guitar tablature. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no app server: the app never sends your data to its developer or to anyone other than Google, and only to store your own files in your own Google Drive.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests two things:

  • Your basic Google profile (name, email address and profile picture). This is used only to show which account you're signed in with.
  • Access to files the app creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file scope). The app creates a “Lyric Chord Composer” folder and saves your compositions there as .hmlcc files. This scope does not let the app see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

Where your data is stored

  • Google Drive — your compositions, in your own account. You own them and can view, download, share or delete them in Drive at any time.
  • Your browser's local storage — a copy of your compositions so the app works quickly, plus display preferences such as light/dark theme. This stays on your device.
  • Memory only — the Google sign-in token, which expires after about an hour. It is never written to disk.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect analytics, use tracking cookies or show ads.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • It does not use your data for any purpose other than letting you edit and save your compositions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third parties

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, and sign-in and storage are provided by Google. Those services handle requests under their own privacy policies: GitHub and Google.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in the app to discard the sign-in token.
  • Delete the “Lyric Chord Composer” folder in Google Drive to remove your compositions.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove the local copy.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
